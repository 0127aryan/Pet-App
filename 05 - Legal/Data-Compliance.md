## A4. Data Compliance Summary (V1)

### Applicable Laws

| Law | Applicability | Our Status |
|---|---|---|
| Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) | Applies — we process Indian users' personal data | Compliant — see Privacy Policy |
| Information Technology Act, 2000 (India) | Applies — we are an Indian intermediary | Compliant — ToU + grievance officer |
| IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021 | Applies — user-generated content platform | Compliant — see below |

### IT Intermediary Guidelines 2021 — Compliance Checklist

| Requirement | Our Implementation |
|---|---|
| Publish Terms of Use, Privacy Policy | ✓ Published at /terms and /privacy |
| Grievance Officer appointed | ✓ Name and contact in Privacy Policy |
| Respond to grievances within 30 days | ✓ Committed in Privacy Policy |
| Remove unlawful content within 36 hours of court/government order | ✓ Admin can remove content immediately |
| Preserve records of removed content for 180 days | Plan: update soft-delete retention to 180 days before launch |
| Not host content that threatens unity/integrity of India | ✓ Covered in prohibited content list |

**Action required before launch:** Update data retention for removed content from 90 days to 180 days to comply with IT Rules 2021.
