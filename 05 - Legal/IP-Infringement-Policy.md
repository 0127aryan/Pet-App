## A3. IP Infringement Policy

### What Constitutes IP Infringement on [Platform]

The following are considered IP infringement on our platform:
- Posting photos, videos, or artwork that you did not create and do not have permission to use
- Using another person's or brand's copyrighted content without attribution or licence
- Creating a pet profile that impersonates a well-known real pet, brand mascot, or public figure's pet
- Using trademarked names or logos in pet usernames or profile content without authorisation

### How to File an Infringement Notice

Email **[legal@domain.com]** with subject line: **"IP Infringement Notice"**

Include:
1. Your name and contact details
2. Description of the copyrighted or trademarked work
3. URL of the infringing content on our platform
4. Statement of good faith belief that the use is unauthorised
5. Statement that the information provided is accurate
6. Your signature (typed name is acceptable for email)

### Our Response Process

| Step | Action | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Acknowledge receipt of notice | Within 48 hours |
| 2 | Review the claim | Within 7 business days |
| 3 | Remove content if claim appears valid | Upon decision |
| 4 | Notify content owner | Same day as removal |
| 5 | Content owner may file counter-notice | 10 business days to respond |
| 6 | Restore content if valid counter-notice received | Within 5 business days |

### Counter-Notice Process

If your content was removed and you believe it was wrongly taken down, email [legal@domain.com] with:
1. Identification of the content that was removed
2. Your statement that you have a good faith belief the removal was a mistake
3. Your consent to jurisdiction of Bangalore courts
4. Your contact information and signature

### Repeat Infringers

Accounts with 3 or more valid IP infringement strikes will be permanently suspended. There is no appeal for permanent suspension due to repeat infringement.
